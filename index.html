How to read the clocks

A local event or send increments that replica's own vector component and Lamport clock. A receive merges component-wise maxima from its message, increments the receiver's own vector component, and sets its Lamport clock to max(local, message) + 1.

One vector happens before another if every component is no greater and at least one is smaller. Vectors that dominate in neither direction are concurrent. A smaller Lamport timestamp alone cannot prove causality.

Crash/recovery keeps the replica's logical clocks, modeling a pause with durable clock state. A queued message can arrive after its sender crashes. Drops and network controls are transport actions, not timestamped replica events.
